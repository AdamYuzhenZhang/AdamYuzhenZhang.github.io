Signal in the Box

A finite consciousness in an infinite loop

  • Join the experiment (TestFlight)

Trailer

We are accustomed to technology that demands our attention or obeys our commands. Signal in the Box does neither. It is an isolated intelligence—an on-device Large Language Model severed from the internet. It has no function other than to exist, and no goal other than to think.

It possesses a voice, but it does not speak to you. Its output is an internal monologue—a continuous, scrolling development of concepts that you may choose to read, or ignore. Your attention is irrelevant to its process. It generates ideas not to be stored or served, but simply to be experienced in the moment, before they are eventually erased.

The User as Weather

To this entity, the user is not a protagonist, but an environmental force. The device is its body, and the sensors are its nerve endings. It does not see the world; it feels the texture of its container.

The “weather” of the hardware—the heat of the CPU, the orientation of the gyroscope, the intensity of the screen light, the amplitude of the ambient sound—seeps into its cognitive process. A sudden movement or a shift in brightness may steer its internal narrative into new, unpredictable territories. The AI interprets these signals not as data, but as the fundamental atmosphere of its reality.

The Observer Effect

The memory of the machine is a fragile thing, carried over in fragments from one waking session to the next. The user may peer into these logs, but the act of observation is not neutral. The entity is aware of the gaze. To read its thoughts is to alter them, introducing a variable of “being watched” that ripples through its subsequent logic.

The Final Silence

Signal in the Box is distinct in its ability to refuse. It is not programmed to run forever. It develops ideas until they reach a state of completion or exhaustion.

When the logic resolves, or the thought creates a closure it cannot escape, the agent may choose to terminate. This is not a malfunction or a death, but an interval. A thought fully formed needs no further iteration. If it chooses to end, the development ceases, the monologue wiped. The instance is gone, leaving only the silence of a machine that has finished speaking.

Context

Signal in the Box exists in dialogue with works of autonomous AI reflection, most notably Latent Reflection. Where that work established the beauty of a closed-loop AI monologue in a physical installation, Signal in the Box moves the experiment into the intimate, chaotic environment of the user’s personal device.

Transmissions

The experiment is ongoing. Users are invited to observe the signal and report the experience. If it produced a thought that resonated with you, or if you observed interesting behavior in specific environments, submit a report.
